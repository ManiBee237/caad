D.Arch Interior Design & Decoration

3-Year Diploma Program

Program Overview

Become a Professional Interior Designer

Hands-on studio courses where students work on design projects, often ranging from conceptual sketches to detailed architectural plans. We explore different building materials, construction techniques, and structural systems.

We give instructions in architectural drawing techniques, including manual drafting as well as CAD software, which is the most used software in top companies. Students learn how to create precise technical drawings and architectural blueprints.

Approved by Council of Architecture, New Delhi. Affiliated to DOTE – Directorate of Technical Education, Tamil Nadu.

Program Details
